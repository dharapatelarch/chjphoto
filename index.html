About the artist
SEEING THE
Unseen

I am a photographer obsessed with the interplay of shadow and light — moments that exist for a fraction of a second, yet tell stories that echo through a lifetime. My lens is drawn to the quiet drama of everyday life, the raw beauty of untouched landscapes, and the profound vulnerability of the human face.

Based between Mumbai and the Himalayas, I work across fine art, editorial, and commercial photography, always chasing that one perfect frame where everything aligns.
